Cricket is a game which lies between the tail of two ends which is bat and ball.
Cricket is a bat-and-ball game played between two teams of 11 players each. The game is played on a large field with a 22-yard pitch in the center.

Basic Rules
One team bats while the other team bowls and fields.
The batting team tries to score runs.
The bowling team tries to dismiss (get out) the batters.
After a set number of overs or when all batters are out, the teams switch roles.
The team with the higher score wins.

Ways to Score Runs
Running between the wickets.
Hitting the ball to the boundary:
4 runs if the ball reaches the boundary after touching the ground.
6 runs if the ball clears the boundary without touching the ground.
Ways a Batter Can Get Out
Bowled
Caught
Run out
LBW (Leg Before Wicket)
Stumped
Main Formats of Cricket
Test Cricket – Played over up to 5 days.
One Day International (ODI) – 50 overs per side.
Twenty20 (T20) – 20 overs per side; the fastest and most exciting format.
